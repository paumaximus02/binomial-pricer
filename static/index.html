Greeks glossary

Price
Fair value of the option in dollars, from the binomial tree.
Delta (Δ)
Change in option price per $1 move in the underlying.
Gamma (Γ)
Change in delta per $1 move in the underlying.
Theta (Θ)
Change in price per year as time passes (usually negative for long options).
Vega (ν)
Change in price per 1.0 absolute move in volatility (e.g. 0.20 → 1.20).
Implied vol
Volatility that makes the model price match the observed market price.
